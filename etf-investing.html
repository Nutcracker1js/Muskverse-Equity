ETF Investing

One purchase, dozens of holdings. Exchange-traded funds let you spread your money across an entire sector or market in a single move — built-in diversification without picking stocks one by one.

Diversification Without the Spreadsheet

Instead of researching and buying ten individual companies, an ETF bundles them into one position you can buy or sell as easily as a single stock. When the basket moves, your investment moves with it. It's the same principle institutional investors have used for decades, scaled down to a position size that fits your account, no matter how big or small you're starting.

One Trade

Exposure to dozens of holdings at once

Lower Risk
