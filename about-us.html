What We Stand For

At every step, our values are in service of the people who invest with us.

SAFETY FIRST

We put reliability before everything else

The reliability of our platform comes first, so we're there when you need us most. We protect your security and privacy, and only share what's necessary to serve your needs. We build safeguards and provide education so you're set up to succeed — and when something isn't right, we fix it. We work closely with regulators to protect you and the broader financial system, and we speak plainly, even when it's not what you want to hear.

EQUAL ACCESS

The rich shouldn't get a better deal

We built this platform because we saw a gap: the more you had, the better deal you got. We believe everyone deserves access to the financial system, regardless of background or account balance. That's why we offer uniform rates, no account minimums, and a product designed from the ground up for accounts of every size.

CUSTOMER-DRIVEN

We exist to make our customers happy

From day one, we've prioritized direct feedback from the people using our platform. Listening to investors shapes every product decision we make. We never settle for "good enough" — we keep asking how to make things better, and we take real pride in the work.

FIRST PRINCIPLES

We make bold bets and question assumptions

We approach problems from first principles — forming a hypothesis, testing it, and reducing complexity to its core parts. We believe the financial system should work for everyone, which is why we build tools that let you start investing at your own pace, on your own terms.
